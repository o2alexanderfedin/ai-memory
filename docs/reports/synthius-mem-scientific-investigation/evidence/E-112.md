## Industry-standard comparable

- **MT-Bench** (Zheng 2023, arXiv:2306.05685): Reports human-judge agreement, typically 80–85% Cohen's κ with LLM judge on pairwise tasks.
- **LoCoMo original** (Maharana et al., 2024): Q-A pairs generated + verified by annotators (line 55 §4.1); downstream F1 scoring is automatic but calibrated against human-annotated evidence spans.

Synthius-Mem paper contains neither.

## Absence quantified

Within the 1,813-question full LoCoMo dataset (line 82 §4.4) × 94.37% accuracy = ~102 total errors. Synthius-Mem does not report human spot-check of:

- Which 2/442 adversarial errors were actual errors vs judge false-positives (§4.4 line 87).
- Which 11/810 core-fact errors were actual errors (§4.4 line 87).
- Any stratified sample of Wrong=0 verdicts (to detect judge false-negatives).

Without a human spot-check, the judge's verdicts are not validated against ground truth beyond the rubric-text level.

## Verdict

**PASS.** Zero hits on all human-validation keywords. Methodological gap G-067 confirmed.

**Recommendation:** A 100-sample stratified-by-category spot-check with 2+ human annotators + Cohen's κ ≥ 0.8 threshold for any re-evaluation. This is the MT-Bench-equivalent bar.
