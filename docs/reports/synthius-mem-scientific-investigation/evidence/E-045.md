- (b) **PASSED** — no recency-primacy commitment either.

Under the pass criterion as written, H-045 fails to find its positive evidence.

This is the classic "paper silence" asymmetry: a disconfirmer-only test would PASS, but a positive-evidence test FAILS. The experiment is legitimately INCONCLUSIVE rather than PASS or FAIL:

- H-045 is not REFUTED: the paper does not commit to recency-primary.
- H-045 is not CONFIRMED: the paper does not commit to confidence-primary either.
- The minimum-information reading is that BOTH H-044 (recency-primary) and H-045 (confidence-primary) are admissible given paper silence.

**OUTCOME: INCONCLUSIVE.** Paper does not commit to either ranking primary. §4.4's "absence of evidence" refusal mechanic is about zero-hit detection, not confidence-primary ranking — my earlier assumption that it maps cleanly onto confidence-primary is not borne out by the prose.
