### 5. Confidence bin mapping (T-008)

Per T-008: confidence ≥ 0.8 → "high", ≥ 0.5 → "medium", < 0.5 → "low". Scores in [0,100] are a SEPARATE axis from confidence, but the same bin mapping is applied to scores for display-quadrant readability (e.g., score 72 with confidence 0.85 renders as "72 [high]" where [high] refers to confidence binning — here the bracket label confusingly conflates with score intensity). **CLARIFICATION NEEDED:** In the drafted block, `[high]/[medium]/[low]` refers to *score* tier not confidence bin. Confidence binning is implicit (top-tier facts with evidence_count≥5 per H-085). A clean render would suffix confidence separately: `O=72[score-high, conf=0.85]`.

The compact form trades clarity for budget. Acceptable under the 1K-token constraint. ⚠️
