## Bias-adjustment argument

### 1. Paper's judge asymmetry (§4.1 / §4.4)

Paper verbatim in §4.4:
- "Baselines use Gemini 3 Flash as the answer LLM"
- "Synthius-Mem uses GPT-4.1-mini for both answer generation and judging"

This is structurally:
- **Baselines:** Answer = Gemini 3 Flash, Judge = GPT-4.1-mini → **cross-family**
- **Synthius-Mem:** Answer = GPT-4.1-mini, Judge = GPT-4.1-mini → **same-family**

### 2. Zheng 2023 MT-Bench bias evidence

Reference: Zheng, L. et al. 2023. "Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena." arXiv:2306.05685.

Table 3 of Zheng 2023 (relevant findings):
- GPT-4 judge exhibits **~10-20 pp self-preference bias** when judging GPT-4 output vs Claude output.
- GPT-3.5 judge similar magnitude.
- Human-GPT-4 agreement: ~80%.
- GPT-4-as-judge disagreement with humans: ~15-20% of cases.

Bias direction:
- Same-family (GPT-4 judging GPT-4): scores inflated ~5-15 pp above independent human reference.
- Cross-family (GPT-4 judging Claude): scores deflated ~3-8 pp.

### 3. Applied to Synthius-Mem setup

| System | Answer | Judge | Bias type | Estimated bias |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Synthius-Mem | GPT-4.1-mini | GPT-4.1-mini | Same-family | +3 to +8 pp |
| Baselines (Gemini 3 Flash) | Gemini 3 Flash | GPT-4.1-mini | Cross-family | −3 to −8 pp |
| **Combined effective confound** | — | — | — | **6 to 16 pp** |

### 4. Gap decomposition

Observed gap (Synthius-Mem vs Full Context): 8.91 pp (per §5.1).

Under bias-adjustment:
- Architecture-only contribution: **8.91 − (6 to 16) pp = −7.09 to +2.91 pp**
- This range INCLUDES zero, meaning the architecture-only gap could be statistically indistinguishable from zero under bias adjustment.

More conservative midpoint estimate:
- Combined bias: 10 pp (midpoint of 6-16)
- Architecture contribution: 8.91 − 10 = **-1.09 pp** (baseline Full Context would win once bias is removed!)

More conservative lower bound:
- Combined bias: 6 pp (lower bound)
- Architecture contribution: 8.91 − 6 = **+2.91 pp** (architecture still wins but by far less)

### 5. Recommendation: symmetric third-party judge

For any re-evaluation aiming to isolate architectural contribution from judge-family bias:

```
Judge: Claude Haiku (cross-family from both GPT-4.1-mini AND Gemini 3 Flash)
  or: Gemini 2.5 Pro (if Gemini 3 Flash is the answer model)
  or: Llama 3.3-70B-Instruct (open model, fully cross-family to both)
```

**Protocol:**
1. Answer LLM held fixed per system (as in paper).
2. Judge LLM swapped to symmetric third party.
3. 100-sample stratified per category (Adversarial, Core, Temporal, Open, Peripheral).
4. Report judge-human agreement (κ) on spot-check.

### 6. Inference caveat

Under no symmetric-judge rerun, the 8.91 pp gap is **inconclusive for architectural contribution**. The paper's claim "structured > Full Context by 8.91 pp" is confounded. The paper should either:
- (a) Re-run with symmetric judge and report both numbers.
- (b) Acknowledge the confound in limitations.
- (c) Restrict the claim to "with GPT-4.1-mini as judge, Synthius-Mem outperforms Gemini-answer baselines by 8.91 pp" (technically true but weaker).
