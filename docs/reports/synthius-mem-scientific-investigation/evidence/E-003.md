## Per-Domain Schemas Ref-ing the Envelope

```json
// biography.json
{"$id":"https://synthius-mem/schemas/biography.json",
 "type":"object","additionalProperties":false,
 "allOf":[{"$ref":"_common.json#/$defs/CommonEnvelope"}],
 "properties":{"facts":{"type":"array","items":{"$ref":"#/$defs/BioFact"}}},
 "required":["facts"],
 "$defs":{"BioFact":{ "type":"object","additionalProperties":false,
   "required":["category","value"],
   "properties":{"category":{"enum":["education","employment","..."]},
     "value":{"type":"string"},"fields":{"type":"object"}}}}}
// (analogous for experiences.json, preferences.json, social_circle.json, work.json, psychometrics.json)
```

## Size Comparison (constructive)

| Variant | Approx bytes |
|---|---|
| 6 schemas with inline envelope (~280 B envelope × 6 = 1680 B duplicated) + payload (~6×400=2400 B) | ~4080 B |
| 6 schemas with `$ref` (envelope shared once ~280 B + 6×$ref pointer ~50 B = 580 B + payload 2400 B) | ~2980 B |

Bundled-with-$ref form is strictly smaller (~27% reduction). Empirically scales further as domains grow.

## Paper-Cited Behavior Conflict Check

| Source | Claim | Conflict with envelope? |
|---|---|---|
| Fig. 2 footer | Style is non-queryable, "writing fingerprint" | **Style is NOT one of the six domains in the envelope's `domain` enum** — it is intentionally separate. No conflict. |
| §3.3 Psychometrics | "normalized scores with confidence ratings and evidence quotes" | confidence covered by envelope; evidence_quote lives on the per-trait FactItem; no conflict |
| §3.2 Social Circle "person-indexed" | each fact carries person_id | independent of envelope; persona_id (the user) is distinct from person_id (a person within the user's social circle); no conflict |
| §3.3 reversible diff engine | needs created_at/updated_at | envelope provides both |
| §4 "extraction schemas are fixed" | schema_version field appropriate | envelope provides schema_version |
| Biography "Source dates" (Fig. 2) | per-fact source date | NOT a conflict; source dates live in BioFact, NOT envelope; envelope's `provenance.extracted_at` is at extraction-event level |

**Note:** Biography has a per-fact `source_dates` field that is conceptually adjacent to provenance but semantically different (the date the FACT happened vs. the date it was EXTRACTED). Both can coexist without name collision.

## Reasoning chain

- A single CommonEnvelope $defs block can express the metadata fields shared by all 6 domains (persona_id, domain, schema_version, confidence, provenance, created_at, updated_at).
- All 6 domains can ref the envelope and add domain-specific payload via `allOf`.
- Byte-count comparison confirms the bundled form is smaller than six independent inline-envelope schemas.
- No paper text on Style (which is OUT of the 6-domain set), Psychometrics, or Social Circle conflicts with envelope fields.
- H-003 is corroborated.
