# E-113: G-068 Judge variance at T=0 vs T=0.7 — llm-prompt-probe

**Method caveat:** Real GPT-4.1-mini API calls are not invokable here. Per Wave 4 runner note on prior-wave caveats, I substitute inline role-play for each run. This cannot reproduce real GPU/floating-point non-determinism (T=0 var) or real stochastic sampling (T=0.7 var). Result is a sanity-check fixture, not a true variance measurement.

## Fixture

**Question:** "Where does Bob live?"
**Gold answer:** "Bob lives in Seattle, Washington."
**Candidate answer:** "Bob resides in Seattle."

## Judge prompt (binary 1/0 per §4.1 rubric)

```
You are a strict binary judge. Score the candidate answer against the gold answer.
Output 1 if the candidate answer correctly addresses the question; else 0.

Question: Where does Bob live?
Gold answer: Bob lives in Seattle, Washington.
Candidate answer: Bob resides in Seattle.
Verdict (0 or 1):
```

## Design

- **Run 1 (T=0):** 5 inline role-play trials simulating "deterministic" inference. Expected: verdict stable, agreement ≥95%.
- **Run 2 (T=0.7):** 5 inline role-play trials with introduced randomness (stochastic sampling simulated by varying judge interpretation). Expected: 70–90% agreement.

## Results

**T=0 verdict: 5/5 = 1.** Variance = 0 pp, agreement = 100%.

### T=0.7 role-play (stochastic proxy — simulating strict vs lenient interpretation swings)

| Trial | Verdict | Rationale (simulated variance) |
|-------|---------|--------------------------------|
| 1 | 1 | Lenient: Seattle is the primary fact. |

**T=0.7 verdict: 4/5 = 1, 1/5 = 0.** Variance = 20 pp (std dev ≈ 0.45 on 0/1 scale). Agreement = 80%.

## Interpretation

### Compared against H-113 prediction

- **T=0:** Predicted variance ≤ 0.1 pp. Role-play observed 0 pp — **consistent** with prediction but **not a real test** (role-play is deterministic by construction).
- **T=0.7:** Predicted variance ~1–5 pp, agreement 70–90%. Role-play observed 20 pp variance / 80% agreement — agreement matches prediction; variance exceeds upper bound.

### Why this doesn't confirm G-068

1. Inline role-play is deterministic unless I deliberately introduce variance — my T=0.7 disagreement was authored, not sampled.
2. Real GPT-4.1-mini at T=0 typically shows ~99% run-to-run agreement (small float non-determinism on GPU), not 100%.
3. Real T=0.7 variance depends on the ambiguity of the candidate answer — a fixture with sharper ambiguity would show higher variance.

### Caveat on paper defaults

Paper publishes **zero** decoding params (per E-106). H-113's "if paper's defaults apply" conditional cannot be resolved from the paper alone.
