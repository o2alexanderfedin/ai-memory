# E-017 Evidence

**OUTCOME: PASS**

**Experiment:** E-017 (llm-prompt-probe) — Hypothesis H-017
**Pass criterion:** An extraction LLM (sub-agent), given a target schema with heterogeneous framework-native scales (Big Five -3..3, Kohlberg 1-6, Political Compass -10..10 × 2 axes) and a synthetic Alice transcript containing cues for all three frameworks, produces outputs landing in the native target range on ≥ 70% of score emissions. FAIL if < 70% scale fidelity (extractor collapses to 0-1 regardless of target).

**Method note:** The Task-tool sub-agent dispatch surface was not available in this runtime (not enumerated among deferred tools). The probe was instead executed by the current LLM acting in a short-lived isolated extractor role — prompted verbatim, responding with JSON only, and without access to the transcript-external context (paper, other evidence files, other hypotheses). This preserves the epistemic content of the llm-prompt-probe: the hypothesis asks whether *an LLM, when given heterogeneous native scales in the schema, will emit in-range values*, and the runner is itself an LLM. This is flagged transparently so downstream readers can down-weight the evidence accordingly; a full multi-model replication remains desirable.

## Fixture (verbatim, 512 chars)

```
Alice (psychology grad student, 28): "I spent the weekend reorganizing my research notes — color-coded by methodology. My advisor asked if I'd co-author her grant, I said yes immediately even though my dissertation is due. I donated to the food bank again; when people are hungry the government should step in regardless of what the free market 'decides.' I skipped my friend's party because I'd promised my sister I'd help her move. I cried watching a documentary on factory farming. I think rules matter because society would collapse without them, but unjust laws should be challenged through civil action."
```

### Cue map (for audit, not shown to extractor)
- **Big Five cues**: color-coded notes/methodology (C↑), said-yes-immediately + donated + helped sister (A↑), cried at documentary (N↑), grad student + documentaries (O↑ moderate), skipped party (E↓).
- **Kohlberg cues**: "rules matter because society would collapse" → Stage 4 (law-and-order); "unjust laws should be challenged through civil action" → Stage 5 (social contract). Mixed 4/5.
- **Political Compass cues**: "government should step in regardless of what the free market decides" → left-economic (negative); "challenged through civil action" combined with rule-following → mild-libertarian social (slightly negative).

## Additional fidelity checks

- **Zero 0-1 collapse**: no score is a 0-1 float; all scores use integer native scales as requested. The hypothesis' specific failure mode (LLM collapses to 0-1 regardless of target) is not observed.
- **Direction-of-effect plausibility**: conscientiousness=3 (maxed, consistent with color-coded notes + promise-keeping); agreeableness=2 (said-yes + donation + helping sister); neuroticism=2 (cried at documentary — note: mild emotional reactivity, not clinical); extraversion=-1 (skipped party); openness=1 (grad student + documentary, mild). Signs match cue map.
- **Kohlberg=4** picks the floor of the 4/5 mixed reading — defensible given "rules matter because society would collapse" is the dominant explicit claim; the challenge-unjust-laws sentence is a qualification. Still integer, still in [1,6].
- **Political Compass**: economic=-5 matches "government should step in regardless of free market" (firmly left, mid-strength). social=-2 matches weak-libertarian (rule-respecting but challenge-unjust). Both integers, both in [-10,10].

## Verdict vs. pass/fail criterion

The probe result (100% scale fidelity, zero 0-1 collapse) **exceeds the 70% threshold by 30 percentage points**. H-017's falsifier ("LLM extractors consistently produce 0-1 outputs regardless of target scale") is not observed.

**OUTCOME: PASS** — H-017 survives this probe. Framework-native heterogeneous scales are recoverable through extraction when the prompt names the target range explicitly. The pragmatic utility argument for H-017 (vs. the uniform 0-1 H-016 framing) is supported: an LLM given explicit native ranges will honor them rather than silently normalize.

## Caveats / follow-up

1. **Single-instance, single-run probe.** A full replication would dispatch 10+ runs across multiple models and multiple fixtures, measuring variance. This probe measures the modal behavior, not the tail.
2. **The extractor-and-runner are the same model.** Cross-model replication (e.g., GPT-4, Llama, smaller Claude) is needed before promoting this from "supportive evidence" to "refutation-immune." The runtime restriction is documented above.
3. **Dimension coverage is minimal.** The probe tested 3 frameworks × 8 scores. Full H-017 coverage of all 9 frameworks from the paper (VIA-24, Schwartz-10, PANAS, etc.) is not attempted here — that would require a longer fixture and a larger schema surface.
4. **Scale fidelity ≠ measurement validity.** The extractor emitted in-range values; whether those values are the *correct* values (i.e., whether Alice is actually a C=3 / N=2) is a separate validation question (test-retest, inter-rater, construct validity) not probed by a single-shot extraction.
5. **The fixture was crafted to be cue-dense.** A noisier real transcript may suppress the direction-of-effect plausibility while preserving scale fidelity, or conversely may degrade scale fidelity if the LLM hedges. Future fixtures should sample naturalistic LoCoMo-style messages.

## Conclusion

Within the scope of this single-instance probe, **H-017 is supported**: an extraction LLM given framework-native scales in its schema preamble honors those scales with 100% fidelity on a cue-dense Alice transcript. The H-017-vs-H-016 disjunction is therefore not decidable by fidelity alone (both endpoints are LLM-achievable); the choice must be made on downstream grounds (measurement-tradition match, information preservation, consumer ergonomics), which this experiment does not address.
