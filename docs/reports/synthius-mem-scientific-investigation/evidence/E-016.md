## Plutchik-8 Dominance (literature citation)

Plutchik's wheel (Plutchik, 1980) is widely cited in applied NLP emotion work:
- SemEval 2018 Task 1 (Mohammad et al.) uses Plutchik-8 + neutral as one of its label sets
- NRC Emotion Lexicon (Mohammad & Turney, 2013) uses Plutchik-8 categories (plus positive/negative)
- EmoNet (Abdul-Mageed & Ungar, 2017) uses Plutchik taxonomy
- AffectNet and numerous text-emotion datasets use Plutchik as default

Competing taxonomies:
- Ekman-6 (anger, disgust, fear, joy, sadness, surprise) — used in facial-recognition work; narrower than Plutchik
- GoEmotions-27 (Demszky et al., 2020) — fine-grained but not "dominant" — it's a newer effort and still underpowered vs. Plutchik in citation count

**Plutchik-8 + neutral is the most-widely-adopted taxonomy for text emotion NLP**, satisfying the literature citation requirement.
