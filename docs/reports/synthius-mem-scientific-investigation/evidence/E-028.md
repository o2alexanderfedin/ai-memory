## Part-of vs. Is-a Semantics

**Part-of (meronymy):** A is-part-of B means A is a component/constituent of B. Breaking a PhD into coursework+viva+publications is part-of decomposition.

**Is-a (taxonomy):** A is-a B means A is a subtype of B. "PhD is an education event" is is-a; "coursework is a PhD" is not is-a, it's part-of.

The drafted schema's `parent_event_id` + `children` encode **part-of**: children are components of parents, not subtypes. Table 1 says "Hierarchical tree" for Experiences with "emotions, intensity, social context" — these are fields of individual events, suggesting tree nodes are events (event part-of event), not taxonomy categories (event is-a category).

§3.3 reinforces: "summarization into biography narratives and life event timelines." "Life event timelines" = sequences of events; this matches part-of decomposition (PhD → coursework → viva → publication is a timeline). Taxonomy (is-a) would not yield a timeline.
