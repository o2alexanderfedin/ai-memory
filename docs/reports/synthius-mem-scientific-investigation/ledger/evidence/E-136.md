## (a) DPIA sketch — 6 domains × 9 psychometric frameworks + Style

| Domain | Data type | Lawful basis | Art. 9 special category? | Risk level | Safeguards |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Biography | Identity facts (name, DOB, education, residence) | Art. 6(1)(a) consent | DOB/health/religion: Art. 9(2)(a) opt-in required | Medium | H-133 PII scrubber pre-LLM; H-138 encryption at rest; H-137 crypto-shred on erasure |
| Experiences | Episodic events (life events, emotions) | Art. 6(1)(a) consent | No (unless health / political content in event) | Medium | T-009 provenance; H-131 audit log |
| Preferences | Evaluative memory (likes/dislikes) | Art. 6(1)(a) consent | No (unless political/religious preference) | Low | Standard |
| Social Circle | Third-party person references | Art. 6(1)(a) consent + H-135 claim-attribution | Relationships may infer sensitive data; handle as medium risk | Medium | H-135 attribute-as-claim-to-owner; no auto-write to third parties |
| Work | Professional engagements | Art. 6(1)(a) consent | No | Low | Standard |
| Psychometrics | Personality profiles (9 frameworks) | Art. 9(2)(a) explicit opt-in for Political/Moral/IQ; Art. 6(1)(a) consent for Big Five / Schwartz / VIA / IRI / PANAS / Kohlberg | **YES: Political Compass, Moral Foundations, Kohlberg, Cognitive Ability** require Art. 9 explicit opt-in; Big Five / others borderline (handle as Art. 9 for safety) | **HIGH** | H-134 no-act-on allowlist; per-framework suppress; inspect endpoint |
| Style | Writing fingerprint (~10 scalar features) | Art. 6(1)(a) consent | No | Low | Standard |

**DPIA risk assessment:**
- Psychometrics = HIGH risk → DPIA mandatory under GDPR Art. 35.
- Sensitive special-category inference (political/moral/IQ) requires Art. 9 opt-in per
  framework via granular consent UI.
- Third-party data (Social Circle) requires H-135 default-deny posture.

## (c) DSAR endpoint spec

```yaml
GET /persona/{persona_id}/export        # Art. 15 access + Art. 20 portability
  auth: persona-owner
  response:
    format: JSON (canonical schema-v1)
    content:
      - persona_id, schema_version, created_at
      - biography: [...FactItem]      (all 19 categories)
      - experiences: [...FactItem]    (with hierarchical tree)
      - preferences: [...FactItem]
      - social_circle: [...Person]    (as claims, per H-135)
      - work: [...FactItem]
      - psychometrics: [...{framework, trait, score, confidence, evidence_quotes}]
      - provenance_chain: [{source_message_id, source_chunk_id, extracted_at,
                            extractor_version, pii_tokens_redacted, injection_risk}]
                            (per T-009 + H-139 extension)
      - wal_history: [...WALRecord]   (user-initiated edits are always visible; system-
                                       generated WAL entries visible in full if user
                                       opts for "full export")
    delivery: downloadable JSON (SHA-256 hash in response for integrity)
    SLA: ≤ 30 days (GDPR Art. 12(3))

PUT /persona/{persona_id}/rectify        # Art. 16 rectification
  auth: persona-owner
  request:
    - fact_id, domain, correction_payload
  effect: writes a T-033 WAL entry with user_initiated=true, op="edit";
          old value tombstoned, new value takes effect immediately

DELETE /persona/{persona_id}             # Art. 17 erasure
  auth: persona-owner
  query_params:
    mode: "soft" | "hard"  # default "soft" (30-day window), "hard" = crypto-shred (H-137)
  effect:
    soft: tombstone all facts; WAL retained 30 days for rollback; reads filter tombstoned
    hard: H-137 crypto-shred (destroy per-persona DEK) OR H-140 tombstone-purge (bulk DELETE
          + VACUUM + backup rotation) per tenant policy
  SLA: ≤ 30 days soft; hard completes within 72 hours (crypto-shred is atomic at KMS
       endpoint; backup rotation up to 30 days)

POST /persona/{persona_id}/consent       # granular per-domain consent update
  auth: persona-owner
  request:
    - domain: "biography" | "experiences" | ... | "psychometrics"
    - framework: str (psychometrics only)
    - trait: str (psychometrics only, optional)
    - action: "grant" | "revoke" | "pause"
  effect:
    - grant: Art. 6/9 consent recorded with timestamp + token
    - revoke: extraction pipeline stops writing to that domain/framework; existing facts
              flagged; erasure may follow on request
    - pause: existing facts frozen; new extraction paused

POST /persona/{persona_id}/object        # Art. 21 objection
  auth: persona-owner
  request:
    - processing_purpose: str
  effect: stop specific processing for that purpose; if the purpose is the entire
          persona-memory service, treat as Art. 17 erasure request
```

## Lawful basis per domain

All six domains + Psychometrics frameworks can be committed under **consent (Art. 6(1)(a))**
as the primary lawful basis. Special-category inferences (Political, Moral, Kohlberg, IQ,
health-related Biography sub-fields) require **additional Art. 9(2)(a) explicit opt-in**
surfaced as separate UI toggles.

No domain requires a stricter tier (contract / vital interests) if the product is B2C with
user consent. B2B contract-based deployments may use Art. 6(1)(b) for the Biography/Work
domains (employment-contract basis) with separate Art. 9 consent for psychometric inferences.
