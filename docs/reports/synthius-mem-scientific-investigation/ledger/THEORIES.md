# Promoted Theories

> Hypotheses that survived ≥1 peer-reviewed SUPPORT verdict with no unrefuted parallel REFUTE. These are the reusable architectural findings the synthesizer will translate into directives.

**Schema:**

| THEORY-ID | From HYPO-ID | GAP-ID | Statement | Evidence chain | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Confidence levels: `HIGH` (multiple independent experiments SUPPORT) / `MEDIUM` (single experiment SUPPORT, no contradicting evidence) / `LOW` (supported but narrowly; recommend targeted re-run later).
